# The Practice — 60 min

**Theme:** Root Chakra (Muladhara)  
**Guiding Principle:** *“When the roots are deep, there is no reason to fear the wind.”*  
**Essence:** Grounded, steady, resilient  
**Peak Pose:** **Malasana (Yogic Squat)** — supported & advanced variations  
**Style:** All-levels Hot Yoga | Hatha → Vinyasa progression  
**Music:** Earthy, grounding, steady build (Hozier-inspired)

---

## 10 Commandments – Through Line

1. **Intention:** Ground down to rise with stability  
2. **Sanskrit:** Simple, consistent pose names (1–2 new terms max)  
3. **Asana:** Standing → squatting → grounding shapes  
4. **Adjustments:** Offered mostly in Round 1 (lower body, pelvis, feet)  
5. **Cueing:** From the ground up; bones → muscles → breath  
6. **Pranayama:** Slow nasal breath → Ujjayi  
7. **Philosophy:** Roots = safety, trust, belonging  
8. **Music:** Felt, not background — tempo mirrors movement  
9. **Connection:** Names used during standing work  
10. **Community:** Warm welcome before + after class  

---

# CLASS FORMAT

## 1. Opening (4–5 min)

- Welcome, name, class format (Hatha → Flow)  
- Consent cards, props, heat awareness  
- **Intention (1):**  
  *“Today we explore grounding — building roots so we can meet life’s winds with steadiness.”*  
- **Philosophy (7):** Briefly share the proverb  
- Invite students to set a personal grounding intention  

---

## 2. Warm Up (8–10 min)

**Goal:** Move spine in all 6 directions + wrists & ankles, establish breath

- **Seated / Tabletop**
  - Cat–Cow *(Marjaryasana–Bitilasana)* — flexion/extension  
  - Seated side bends *(Parsva Sukhasana)* — lateral flexion  
  - Seated or tabletop twists *(Parivrtta)* — rotation  
- **Wrists & Ankles**
  - Wrist circles, ankle rolls, toe tucks  
- **Pranayama (6):**
  - Slow nasal breathing → gentle **Ujjayi**  
  - Cue: *“Feel the breath drop into the base of the body.”*  
- Music: Low, steady, earthy  

---

## 3. Energetic Body (30–35 min)

### Sequence 1: Grounding Foundations

**Focus:** Feet, legs, stability

**Round 1 — Hatha (3–4 breaths each):**
- Mountain *(Tadasana)*  
- Forward Fold *(Uttanasana)* — blocks  
- Half Lift *(Ardha Uttanasana)*  
- Low Lunge *(Anjaneyasana)* — knee down  
- **Adjustments:** Feet rooting, pelvic stacking  

**Round 2 — Vinyasa (1 breath / 1 movement):**
- Same shapes → smooth transitions  
- End with **Vinyasa** *(Plank–Chaturanga–Up Dog–Down Dog)*  

---

### Sequence 2: Strengthening the Roots

**Focus:** Legs, hips, inner stability

**Round 1 — Hatha:**
- Chair *(Utkatasana)*  
- Warrior II *(Virabhadrasana II)*  
- Extended Side Angle *(Utthita Parsvakonasana)* — forearm/block  
- **Cueing:** Press through heels, hug muscles to bones  

**Round 2 — Vinyasa:**
- Flow through sequence with breath-led cadence  
- End with **Vinyasa**  

---

### Sequence 3: Peak – Malasana

**Focus:** Grounded surrender

**Round 1 — Hatha:**
- **Malasana (Yogic Squat)**
  - **Beginner:** Bolster/block under hips, heels elevated  
  - **Advanced:** Bound Malasana, twist *(Parivrtta Malasana)*  
- Crow prep option *(Bakasana)* for advanced students  
- **Adjustments:** Pelvis, spine length, heel grounding  

**Round 2 — Vinyasa:**
- Flow into Malasana → fold → **Vinyasa**  

*Music here is strongest, earthy, rhythmic.*

---

## 4. Cool Down (8–10 min)

**Goal:** Down-regulate, integrate, prepare for rest

- Seated Forward Fold *(Paschimottanasana)* — bolster  
- Supine Twist *(Supta Matsyendrasana)*  
- Happy Baby *(Ananda Balasana)*  
- **Pranayama:** Natural breath, longer exhales  
- **Philosophy:**  
  *“When we feel supported by the ground, the nervous system softens.”*  
- Music slows, softens  

---

## 5. Closing (5 min)

- **Savasana** — eye masks offered  
- Guided body awareness at the base of the spine  
- **Seal the Practice:**
  - Return to the proverb  
  - Invite students to carry grounded resilience off the mat  
- Close with gratitude + clear ending  

---

## Community (Before & After)

- Greet students by name  
- Ask how their body feels post-practice  
- Thank them for sharing energy in the room
