# Solar Plexus Chakra — Manipura
**60‑Minute Practice**

**Theme:** Inner fire, self‑trust, decisive action  
**Mantra:** *I trust myself.*  
**Element:** Fire  
**Quality:** Focused, contained heat

**Opening Quote (choose one):**  
- “Trust yourself. You know more than you think you do.” — Benjamin Spock  
- “Confidence is quiet. Insecurities are loud.” — Unknown  
- “With realization of one’s own potential and self‑confidence in one’s ability, one can build a better world.” — Dalai Lama

**Chakra Overview (brief):**  
Manipura lives at the solar plexus—the center of will, digestion, and personal power. This practice gathers energy, directs it clearly, and builds trust through steady effort and containment.

---

## Warm Up (8–10 min)
*Goal: wake the core, build heat gradually, introduce twists*

- Savasana → hands on upper belly, steady nasal breath  
- Knees‑to‑Chest → small core engagement on exhale  
- Supine Twist (L / R)  
- Dead Bug (slow, opposite arm/leg)  
- Roll up to Seated → Seated Twist (L / R)  
- Tabletop → Bird Dog (hold, switch)  
- Plank → slow lower to belly  
- Low Cobra (Bhujangasana), 2–3 rounds  
- Down Dog → walk to top of mat

*Transition cue:* “We’re awake now—let’s build.”

---

## Sequence 1 — Build the Fire
*Focus: legs + core integration, steady effort*

- Chair (Utkatasana)  
- Chair Twist (Parivrtta Utkatasana)  
- High Lunge (Anjaneyasana)  
- High Lunge Twist (Parivrtta Anjaneyasana)  
- Warrior I (Virabhadrasana I)  
→ Vinyasa

Repeat second side, slightly quicker breath‑to‑movement.

---

## Sequence 2 — Contain & Direct
*Focus: strength, balance, mental focus*

- Warrior II (Virabhadrasana II)  
- Reverse Warrior  
- Extended Side Angle (Utthita Parsvakonasana)  
- Half Moon (Ardha Chandrasana) — block option  
- Warrior III (Virabhadrasana III)  
→ Vinyasa

*Cue:* “Draw energy into the center. Stay steady.”

---

## Sequence 3 — Peak: Core & Will
*Focus: commitment, inner strength, staying power*

- Plank Hold  
- Side Plank (Vasisthasana) — optional variation  
- Boat (Navasana)  
- Low Boat → High Boat (dynamic)  
- Seated Twist (Parivrtta Navasana or simple twist)

Optional repeat of Boat, then release.

---

## Cool Down (8–10 min)
*Goal: release heat without collapsing energy*

- Seated Forward Fold (Paschimottanasana)  
- Supine Twist (L / R)  
- Reclined Figure‑4  
- Happy Baby  
- Gentle belly breathing with longer exhales

*Cue:* “The fire has done its work. Let it settle.”

---

## Closing
- Savasana  
- Brief reflection at the solar plexus:  
  “Notice what it feels like to trust your own strength.”  
- Seal with mantra: *I trust myself.*
