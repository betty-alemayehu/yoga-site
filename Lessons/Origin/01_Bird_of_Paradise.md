# The Origin — 60 min Workshop

**Peak Pose:** *Svarga Dvijasana* (Bird of Paradise)  
**Theme:** *Svadhisthana* (Sacral Chakra) — Creativity, Fluidity, Opening  
**Secondary Themes:** Balance & Drishti | Blooming & Unfolding  
**Guiding Principle:** *"Like the bird of paradise flower, we unfold one petal at a time — patient, rooted, and radiant."*  
**Style:** Level 1 Foundations | Hatha | Workshop-Style  
**Setting:** Bright room, no music, space for questions  
**Props:** Strap, 2 blocks, bolster, wall access

---

| Section | Time | Focus |
|---------|------|-------|

# CLASS FORMAT

## 1. Opening Meditation + Breathwork (5-7 min)

### Welcome & Setup
- Greet students by name as they enter
- Invite students to gather props: **1 strap, 2 blocks, 1 bolster**
- Explain workshop format: *"This is a space to learn, pause, and ask questions. We'll move slowly and build toward our peak pose together."*
- Consent for hands-on adjustments (verbal check-in or cards)

### Seated Meditation (2 min)
- *Sukhasana* (Easy Seat) on bolster or blocks for hip elevation
- Eyes closed or soft gaze downward
- Cue: *"Feel the sit bones grounding. Let the spine rise naturally from that foundation."*
- Invite a personal intention related to **openness, creativity, or patience**

### *Nadi Shodhana Pranayama* — Alternate Nostril Breathing (3-4 min)

**Origin & Meaning:**
> *Nadi* = energy channel; *Shodhana* = purification  
> This practice balances the *Ida* (lunar/cooling) and *Pingala* (solar/warming) energy channels, calming the nervous system and preparing the mind for focused practice. Rooted in Hatha Yoga traditions, it is considered one of the most balancing pranayama techniques.

**Setup & Instructions:**
1. Right hand in *Vishnu Mudra* (index + middle fingers folded to palm)
2. Left hand rests on left knee, palm up or in *Chin Mudra*
3. **Cycle:**
   - Close right nostril with thumb → inhale left (4 counts)
   - Close both nostrils → pause (2 counts)
   - Release right nostril → exhale right (4 counts)
   - Inhale right (4 counts)
   - Close both → pause (2 counts)
   - Release left → exhale left (4 counts)
4. Repeat 4-6 rounds
5. Release mudra, return to natural breath

**Teaching Notes:**
- Demonstrate hand position clearly
- Pause for questions on technique
- Offer modification: visualize breath alternating if hand position is uncomfortable

## 2. Theory & Philosophy (5-8 min)

### *Svadhisthana* — The Sacral Chakra

**Sanskrit Breakdown:**
> *Sva* = self; *Adhisthana* = dwelling place  
> "The dwelling place of the self"

**Location:** Lower abdomen, below the navel, including hips and pelvis

**Qualities:**
- **Element:** Water (*Jala*) — fluidity, adaptability, flow
- **Color:** Orange
- **Themes:** Creativity, sensuality, emotional intelligence, pleasure, change
- **When balanced:** Ease in movement, creative expression, healthy boundaries, emotional resilience
- **When blocked:** Rigidity (physical and emotional), fear of change, hip tightness

**Connection to *Svarga Dvijasana*:**
> Bird of Paradise requires deep hip opening and the willingness to unfold — to move from a bound, protected shape into full extension. This mirrors the sacral chakra's invitation to release rigidity and trust the flow.

### The Pose: *Svarga Dvijasana* (Bird of Paradise)

**Sanskrit Breakdown:**
> *Svarga* = heaven, paradise; *Dvija* = twice-born (bird); *Asana* = pose  
> Named after the bird of paradise flower — vibrant, exotic, unfolding in layers

**Symbolism:**
- The pose begins **bound and compact** (like a bud)
- Through patience and balance, it **unfolds into full bloom**
- Teaches us that opening is a process, not a destination

**Key Actions:**
- Hip opening (external rotation of bound leg)
- Hamstring lengthening (extended leg)
- Balance and *Drishti* (steady gaze, steady mind)
- Core engagement for stability

*Pause for questions before moving into physical practice.*

## 3. Warm-Up / Prep Sequence (18-20 min)

**Goal:** Prepare hips, hamstrings, shoulders, and balance for peak pose

### A. Grounding & Spine Awakening (4 min)

**Tabletop — *Bharmanasana***
- Wrist circles, ankle rolls
- *Marjaryasana-Bitilasana* (Cat-Cow) — 5-6 rounds
  - Cue: *"Initiate from the pelvis. Let the sacral area lead the movement."*

**Thread the Needle — *Parsva Balasana***
- Hold each side 5 breaths
- Opens shoulders for bind prep

---

### B. Hip Opening Sequence (8 min)

**Low Lunge — *Anjaneyasana***
- Back knee down, blanket for padding if needed
- **Prop Setup:** Block under front hand if needed for balance
- Hold 5-6 breaths each side
- Cue: *"Sink the hips forward and down. Breathe into the front of the back hip."*

**Lizard Pose — *Utthan Pristhasana***
- Front foot to outer edge of mat
- **Beginner:** Hands on blocks (high or medium height), back knee down
- **Intermediate:** Forearms on blocks or floor, back knee lifted
- **Advanced:** Forearm to floor, back knee lifted, optional quad stretch (bend back knee, reach for foot)
- Hold 8-10 breaths each side

**Common Mistakes:**
- Front knee collapsing inward → Cue: *"Press knee toward pinky toe"*
- Rounding spine → Cue: *"Lengthen from tailbone to crown"*
- Holding breath → Cue: *"Keep the breath soft and steady"*

**Adjustment Opportunities:**
- *Hands-on:* Gentle pressure on sacrum to encourage forward tilt (with consent)
- *Verbal:* "Imagine the hip bones pouring forward like water"

---

**Half Splits — *Ardha Hanumanasana***
- From Low Lunge, shift hips back, straighten front leg
- **Prop Setup:** Blocks under hands, flex front foot
- Hold 5-6 breaths each side
- Cue: *"Hinge from the hips, not the waist. Keep spine long."*

**Common Mistakes:**
- Rounding back to reach toes → Cue: *"Flat back is more important than depth"*
- Locking front knee → Cue: *"Micro-bend to protect the joint"*

---

**Wide-Legged Forward Fold — *Prasarita Padottanasana***
- Feet parallel, 3-4 feet apart
- **Prop Setup:** Blocks under hands, or hands to floor
- Hold 8-10 breaths
- Cue: *"Root through outer edges of feet. Let the head be heavy."*

**Adjustment Opportunities:**
- *Hands-on:* Gentle traction on hips, drawing them back (with consent)
- *Verbal:* "Shift weight slightly forward into the balls of the feet"

---

### C. Bind Preparation (4 min)

**Bound Extended Side Angle — *Baddha Utthita Parsvakonasana***

This is the **foundation shape** for Bird of Paradise.

**Progression:**

**Level 1 — With Strap (Beginners):**
1. From Warrior II (*Virabhadrasana II*), right leg forward
2. Lower right forearm to right thigh (or block inside front foot)
3. **Strap Setup:** Hold strap in left hand, drape behind back
4. Right hand reaches under right thigh, grasps strap
5. Work hands closer on strap over time
6. Hold 5-6 breaths, repeat other side

**Level 2 — Half Bind:**
1. Left arm wraps behind back, hand toward right hip
2. Right arm reaches under right thigh, fingertips reach toward left hand
3. Hands may not connect — that's okay
4. Hold 5-6 breaths

**Level 3 — Full Bind (Advanced):**
1. Hands clasp behind right thigh
2. Keep chest open, gaze up or forward
3. Hold 5-6 breaths

**Common Mistakes:**
- Collapsing chest toward floor → Cue: *"Rotate chest toward ceiling"*
- Front knee caving in → Cue: *"Knee tracks over second toe"*
- Forcing the bind → Cue: *"Use the strap. The bind will come with time."*

**Adjustment Opportunities:**
- *Hands-on:* Support under front thigh to help student find bind; gentle rotation of top shoulder back (with consent)
- *Verbal:* "Soften the grip. Let the shoulders release down the back."

---

### D. Balance Prep (2 min)

**Tree Pose — *Vrksasana***
- **Prop Setup:** Hand to wall for support if needed
- Focus on *Drishti* (steady gaze point)
- Hold 5-6 breaths each side
- Cue: *"The gaze steadies the mind. The mind steadies the body."*

---

## 4. Peak Pose Progression: *Svarga Dvijasana* (12-15 min)

### Entering the Pose — Step by Step

**Starting Position:** *Baddha Utthita Parsvakonasana* (Bound Extended Side Angle) — right leg forward

---

### Progression Level 1: Standing Bound Pose (Beginner)

**For students using a strap or working on the bind**

1. From Bound Side Angle, shift weight into left (back) foot
2. Begin to straighten left leg, lifting torso upright
3. Right foot stays on the floor OR lifts just slightly
4. **Strap remains in hands** — no need to extend leg
5. Focus: **Balance and upright spine**
6. Hold 5-8 breaths
7. **Wall Option:** Stand with left shoulder near wall for support

**Cues:**
- *"Root down through the standing leg. Feel the floor."*
- *"Stack shoulders over hips. No need to rush."*

---

### Progression Level 2: Knee Lifted (Intermediate)

1. From standing bound position, lift right knee toward chest
2. Keep the bind (strap or hands)
3. Right foot is OFF the floor, knee bent
4. Torso stays upright, core engaged
5. Hold 5-8 breaths

**Cues:**
- *"Hug the lifted knee in. Engage your low belly."*
- *"Find your Drishti — one steady point."*

**Common Mistakes:**
- Leaning torso forward → Cue: *"Lift the chest, stack the spine"*
- Gripping with shoulders → Cue: *"Soften shoulders away from ears"*
- Holding breath → Cue: *"Breathe into the challenge"*

---

### Progression Level 3: Leg Extended (Intermediate-Advanced)

1. From knee-lifted position, begin to extend right leg out to the side
2. Leg does NOT need to be straight — partial extension is valid
3. **Strap users:** Walk hands further apart on strap to allow extension
4. Keep chest lifted, shoulders back
5. Hold 5-8 breaths

**Cues:**
- *"Extend only as far as you can while keeping the chest open."*
- *"The leg doesn't need to be straight. Honor where you are today."*

**Adjustment Opportunities:**
- *Hands-on:* Support under extended thigh to reduce load; gentle guidance on hip rotation (with consent)
- *Verbal:* "Press through the ball of the extended foot, even in the air"

---

### Progression Level 4: Full Expression (Advanced)

1. Full bind (no strap), leg fully extended
2. Chest broad, gaze forward or up
3. Standing leg strong, micro-bend in knee
4. Hold 5-8 breaths

**Cues:**
- *"Bloom open. Let the pose radiate from your center."*
- *"Stay soft in the face. This is still a practice, not a performance."*

---

### Exiting the Pose

1. Bend extended leg, draw knee back in
2. With control, lower foot to floor
3. Release bind, return to *Tadasana* (Mountain Pose)
4. Pause. Notice sensations.
5. **Repeat entire sequence on left side**

---

### Teaching Notes for Peak Pose

**Pause Points:**
- After demonstrating each progression, pause for questions
- Walk the room and offer individual guidance
- Remind students: *"There is no 'full pose.' Every stage IS the pose."*

**Common Mistakes Summary:**
| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| Rounding spine forward | Lift chest, engage core |
| Locking standing knee | Micro-bend for stability |
| Forcing leg straight | Bend knee, prioritize balance |
| Losing Drishti | Re-establish gaze point |
| Gripping strap too tightly | Soften hands, relax shoulders |

**Adjustment Opportunities Summary:**
- *Hands-on:* Support thigh, guide hip rotation, stabilize standing hip (always with consent)
- *Verbal:* Drishti reminders, breath cues, permission to use wall/strap

---

## 5. Cool Down (8-10 min)

**Goal:** Release hips and hamstrings, down-regulate nervous system

### Reclined Pigeon — *Supta Kapotasana* (Figure Four)
- On back, right ankle crosses over left thigh
- **Prop Setup:** Strap around left thigh if hands don't reach
- Hold 8-10 breaths each side
- Cue: *"Let gravity do the work. Soften the effort."*

### Reclined Hand-to-Big-Toe — *Supta Padangusthasana*
- Strap around right foot, extend leg toward ceiling
- **Variation:** Open leg out to right side for inner thigh release
- Hold 6-8 breaths each position, each side
- Cue: *"Keep both hips grounded. Let the strap be an extension of your arm."*

### Supine Twist — *Supta Matsyendrasana*
- Knees to chest, drop both knees to right
- Arms wide, gaze left (or neutral)
- **Prop Setup:** Bolster or block between/under knees if needed
- Hold 8-10 breaths each side
- Cue: *"Breathe into the space between the ribs. Let the exhale release tension."*

### Happy Baby — *Ananda Balasana*
- Grab outer edges of feet or use strap around feet
- Gentle rock side to side
- Hold 6-8 breaths
- Cue: *"Return to that quality of play. Soften the face."*

---

## 6. Savasana + Closing (5-7 min)

### Setup
- *Savasana* (Corpse Pose)
- **Props:** Bolster under knees, blanket for warmth, eye covering offered
- Cue: *"Let the body be heavy. Let the floor hold you completely."*

### Guided Rest (3-4 min)
- Draw attention to the **sacral area** — lower belly, hips, pelvis
- *"Notice any warmth, openness, or release in this area."*
- *"Allow the breath to be natural. Nothing to fix, nowhere to go."*
- Silence for 1-2 minutes

### Closing (2 min)
- Invite small movements — fingers, toes
- Knees to chest, roll to right side in fetal position
- *"Honor this shape — the beginning of all things."*
- Rise to seated

### Seal the Practice
- Hands to heart in *Anjali Mudra*
- Return to the guiding principle:
  > *"Like the bird of paradise flower, we unfold one petal at a time — patient, rooted, and radiant."*
- *"Carry this patience and openness with you off the mat."*
- Close with *"Namaste"* or moment of gratitude

- Offer to demonstrate or revisit any progression
- Remind them: *"This pose is a journey. Come back to it with fresh eyes each time."*
