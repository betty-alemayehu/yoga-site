# The Origin — 60 min Workshop

**Peak Pose:** *Parivrtta Svarga Dvijasana* (Revolved Bird of Paradise)  
**Theme:** *Svadhisthana* (Sacral Chakra) — Creativity, Fluidity, Transformation  
**Secondary Themes:** Balance & Drishti | Blooming Through Rotation  
**Guiding Principle:** *"To twist is to wring out the old and make space for the new. We revolve to evolve."*  
**Style:** Level 1 Foundations | Hatha | Workshop-Style  
**Setting:** Bright room, no music, space for questions  
**Props:** Strap, 2 blocks, bolster, wall access

# CLASS FORMAT

## 1. Opening Meditation + Breathwork (5-7 min)

### Welcome & Setup
- Greet students by name as they enter
- Invite students to gather props: **1 strap, 2 blocks, 1 bolster**
- Explain workshop format: *"Today we explore a twist on a familiar shape. We'll build slowly, layer by layer, with space to pause and ask questions."*
- Consent for hands-on adjustments (verbal check-in or cards)

### Seated Meditation (2 min)
- *Sukhasana* (Easy Seat) on bolster or blocks for hip elevation
- Eyes closed or soft gaze downward
- Cue: *"Ground through the sit bones. Feel the natural curves of the spine stacking."*
- Invite a personal intention related to **transformation, release, or making space**

### *Nadi Shodhana Pranayama* — Alternate Nostril Breathing (3-4 min)

**Origin & Meaning:**
> *Nadi* = energy channel; *Shodhana* = purification  
> This practice balances the *Ida* (lunar/cooling) and *Pingala* (solar/warming) energy channels. In the context of twisting, *Nadi Shodhana* prepares the body and mind for the wringing, cleansing action of spinal rotation — clearing stagnant energy and creating balance.

**Setup & Instructions:**
1. Right hand in *Vishnu Mudra* (index + middle fingers folded to palm)
2. Left hand rests on left knee, palm up or in *Chin Mudra*
3. **Cycle:**
   - Close right nostril with thumb → inhale left (4 counts)
   - Close both nostrils → pause (2 counts)
   - Release right nostril → exhale right (4 counts)
   - Inhale right (4 counts)
   - Close both → pause (2 counts)
   - Release left → exhale left (4 counts)
4. Repeat 4-6 rounds
5. Release mudra, return to natural breath

**Teaching Notes:**
- Demonstrate hand position clearly
- Pause for questions on technique
- Offer modification: visualize breath alternating if hand position is uncomfortable
- Connect to theme: *"We balance left and right, just as we'll balance twist and counter-twist in our practice."*

## 2. Theory & Philosophy (5-8 min)

### *Svadhisthana* — The Sacral Chakra

**Sanskrit Breakdown:**
> *Sva* = self; *Adhisthana* = dwelling place  
> "The dwelling place of the self"

**Location:** Lower abdomen, below the navel, including hips and pelvis

**Qualities:**
- **Element:** Water (*Jala*) — fluidity, adaptability, flow
- **Color:** Orange
- **Themes:** Creativity, emotional release, transformation, letting go
- **When balanced:** Ease in movement, ability to adapt, emotional flow
- **When blocked:** Rigidity, resistance to change, holding patterns in hips and low back

**Connection to Twisting:**
> Twists are often called the "detoxifying" poses — not just physically, but energetically. When we twist, we compress and then release, creating space for fresh *prana* (life force) to flow. The sacral chakra governs our ability to let go and adapt. Revolved Bird of Paradise asks us to find stability while spiraling — to hold our center even as we transform.

### The Pose: *Parivrtta Svarga Dvijasana* (Revolved Bird of Paradise)

**Sanskrit Breakdown:**
> *Parivrtta* = revolved, twisted; *Svarga* = heaven, paradise; *Dvija* = twice-born (bird); *Asana* = pose  
> A twisted variation of Bird of Paradise — the flower spiraling toward light

**Symbolism:**
- The standard Bird of Paradise unfolds outward
- The **revolved** version adds a spiral — representing **evolution through rotation**
- Teaches us that growth isn't always linear; sometimes we must turn inward to expand outward

**Key Actions:**
- Deep hip opening (standing leg and bound leg)
- Spinal rotation (thoracic twist)
- Hamstring lengthening
- Balance, core engagement, and *Drishti*
- **Opposite bind** from standard Bird of Paradise

**Key Difference from Standard Bird of Paradise:**
> In the standard pose, you bind under the SAME-SIDE leg (right arm under right thigh). In the revolved version, you bind under the OPPOSITE leg (right arm under LEFT thigh), creating the twist.

*Pause for questions before moving into physical practice.*

## 3. Warm-Up / Prep Sequence (18-20 min)

**Goal:** Prepare hips, hamstrings, spine for rotation, and introduce the revolved bind

### A. Grounding & Spine Awakening (4 min)

**Tabletop — *Bharmanasana***
- Wrist circles, ankle rolls
- *Marjaryasana-Bitilasana* (Cat-Cow) — 5-6 rounds
  - Cue: *"Feel the spine as a wave. Initiate from the pelvis, let it ripple through."*

**Thread the Needle — *Parsva Balasana***
- From tabletop, right arm threads under left arm
- Right shoulder and temple to floor
- **Prop Setup:** Block under head if shoulder is tight
- Hold 5-6 breaths each side
- Cue: *"Let the twist come from the mid-back, not just the shoulder."*

**Adjustment Opportunities:**
- *Hands-on:* Gentle pressure on upper back to deepen rotation (with consent)
- *Verbal:* "Keep hips stacked over knees"

---

### B. Twisting Sequence (6 min)

**Revolved Low Lunge — *Parivrtta Anjaneyasana***
- From Low Lunge (right foot forward), back knee down
- Hands to prayer at heart center
- Twist right, hooking left elbow outside right knee
- **Prop Setup:** Block under bottom hand if needed; blanket under back knee
- Hold 5-6 breaths each side

**Progression:**
- **Level 1:** Hands at heart, gentle twist
- **Level 2:** Bottom hand to block, top arm extends
- **Level 3:** Bottom hand to floor inside front foot, top arm reaches

**Common Mistakes:**
- Rounding spine to force twist → Cue: *"Lengthen first, then rotate"*
- Front knee collapsing → Cue: *"Knee tracks over second toe"*
- Holding breath → Cue: *"Inhale to lengthen, exhale to twist deeper"*

**Adjustment Opportunities:**
- *Hands-on:* One hand on sacrum, one on upper back to guide rotation (with consent)
- *Verbal:* "Draw the belly in to support the twist"

---

**Revolved Triangle — *Parivrtta Trikonasana***
- From standing, step right foot forward, left foot back (shorter stance than Warrior)
- Square hips forward
- Hinge at hips, place left hand to block (inside or outside right foot)
- Right arm extends to ceiling, twist through thoracic spine
- **Prop Setup:** Block on highest height to start; lower as flexibility allows
- Hold 5-6 breaths each side

**Common Mistakes:**
- Hips rotating open with the twist → Cue: *"Keep hips square, twist from ribcage"*
- Locking front knee → Cue: *"Micro-bend to protect the joint"*
- Collapsing chest → Cue: *"Reach through both arms equally"*

**Adjustment Opportunities:**
- *Hands-on:* Stabilize hip with one hand, guide shoulder rotation with other (with consent)
- *Verbal:* "Imagine the spine as a spiral staircase — each vertebra rotates slightly"

---

### C. Hip Opening Sequence (5 min)

**Lizard Pose — *Utthan Pristhasana***
- Front foot to outer edge of mat
- **Beginner:** Hands on blocks, back knee down
- **Intermediate:** Forearms on blocks, back knee lifted
- **Advanced:** Forearms to floor, optional quad stretch
- Hold 8-10 breaths each side

**Twisted Lizard Variation:**
- From Lizard (right foot forward), keep left hand grounded
- Rotate torso right, right arm reaches to ceiling
- Hold 4-5 breaths
- Cue: *"Ground through the left hand to create leverage for the twist."*

---

**Wide-Legged Forward Fold with Twist — *Parivrtta Prasarita Padottanasana***
- Feet wide, parallel
- Fold forward, place left hand to center (on block or floor)
- Right arm extends to ceiling, twist through mid-back
- Hold 5-6 breaths each side
- Cue: *"Keep both legs equally active. Root through the outer edges of the feet."*

---

### D. Revolved Bind Preparation (5 min)

**Revolved Extended Side Angle — *Parivrtta Utthita Parsvakonasana***

This is the **foundation shape** for Revolved Bird of Paradise.

**Setup (Right Leg Forward):**
1. From Warrior I (*Virabhadrasana I*), right leg forward
2. Bring hands to prayer at heart
3. Twist left, hooking left elbow outside right knee
4. This is the **base position** — pause here to establish the twist

**Progression to Bind:**

**Level 1 — Prayer Twist (Beginners):**
- Hands stay at heart in prayer
- Focus on length in spine and depth of twist
- Hold 5-6 breaths
- Cue: *"Press palms together to help rotate the chest."*

**Level 2 — Open Arms:**
- From prayer twist, extend arms — left fingertips to floor (or block), right arm to ceiling
- Hold 5-6 breaths
- Cue: *"Stack shoulders vertically. Gaze follows top hand."*

**Level 3 — Half Bind with Strap (Intermediate):**
1. From open arms, right arm wraps behind back toward left hip
2. **Strap Setup:** Hold strap in right hand
3. Left arm threads UNDER right thigh (this is the key difference from standard Bird of Paradise)
4. Left hand grasps strap behind right thigh
5. Work hands closer on strap over time
6. Hold 5-6 breaths

**Level 4 — Full Bind (Advanced):**
1. Hands clasp behind right thigh (right arm behind back, left arm under thigh)
2. Keep chest rotating toward ceiling
3. Hold 5-6 breaths

**Common Mistakes:**
- Losing the twist to reach for bind → Cue: *"Twist first, bind second. Never sacrifice rotation for grip."*
- Back heel lifting → Cue: *"Ground the back heel or shorten your stance"*
- Rounding spine → Cue: *"Lengthen from tailbone to crown before deepening"*

**Adjustment Opportunities:**
- *Hands-on:* Guide strap placement; support under threading arm; gentle rotation of upper back (with consent)
- *Verbal:* "The bind is a bonus. The twist is the pose."

---

## 4. Peak Pose Progression: *Parivrtta Svarga Dvijasana* (12-15 min)

### Understanding the Revolved Bind

**Key Difference Reminder:**
> Standard Bird of Paradise: Same-side bind (right arm under right thigh)  
> **Revolved** Bird of Paradise: **Opposite-side bind** (left arm under right thigh, right arm behind back) — this creates the twist

### Entering the Pose — Step by Step

**Starting Position:** *Parivrtta Utthita Parsvakonasana* with bind (Revolved Extended Side Angle) — right leg forward, left arm under right thigh, right arm behind back

---

### Progression Level 1: Standing Revolved Bound Pose (Beginner)

**For students using a strap or working on the bind**

1. From Revolved Side Angle with bind, shift weight into LEFT (back) foot
2. Begin to straighten left leg, lifting torso upright
3. Right foot stays on the floor OR lifts just slightly
4. **Maintain the twist** — chest rotates toward the right
5. **Strap remains in hands** — no need to extend leg
6. Focus: **Balance, upright spine, maintaining rotation**
7. Hold 5-8 breaths
8. **Wall Option:** Stand with back near wall for support

**Cues:**
- *"Root down through the standing leg. The twist stays with you as you rise."*
- *"Keep rotating the chest open, even as you stand."*

---

### Progression Level 2: Knee Lifted with Twist (Intermediate)

1. From standing revolved bound position, lift right knee toward chest
2. Keep the bind (strap or hands) and the twist
3. Right foot is OFF the floor, knee bent
4. Torso stays upright AND rotated
5. Hold 5-8 breaths

**Cues:**
- *"The twist wants to unwind — resist it. Keep spiraling."*
- *"Engage the low belly to support both balance and rotation."*
- *"Find your Drishti — one steady point, even as you spiral."*

**Common Mistakes:**
- Losing the twist as you stand → Cue: *"Maintain chest rotation throughout"*
- Leaning forward → Cue: *"Stack shoulders over hips"*
- Gripping with shoulders → Cue: *"Soften the tops of the shoulders"*

---

### Progression Level 3: Leg Extended with Twist (Intermediate-Advanced)

1. From knee-lifted position, begin to extend right leg out to the side
2. **Maintain the twist** — this is the challenge
3. Leg does NOT need to be straight — partial extension is valid
4. **Strap users:** Walk hands further apart on strap to allow extension
5. Keep chest rotating toward the right (direction of bound leg)
6. Hold 5-8 breaths

**Cues:**
- *"Extend only as far as you can while keeping the spiral."*
- *"The twist is more important than the leg extension."*
- *"Breathe into the space you're creating."*

**Adjustment Opportunities:**
- *Hands-on:* Support under extended thigh; gentle guidance to maintain rotation; stabilize standing hip (with consent)
- *Verbal:* "Imagine wringing out a towel — the twist compresses and creates space"

---

### Progression Level 4: Full Expression (Advanced)

1. Full bind (no strap), leg fully extended
2. Deep twist maintained — chest faces opposite direction of extended leg
3. Standing leg strong, micro-bend in knee
4. Gaze follows the twist (over shoulder or forward for balance)
5. Hold 5-8 breaths

**Cues:**
- *"Spiral from the navel. Let the twist originate from your center."*
- *"This is evolution through rotation. You are transforming in the pose."*
- *"Stay soft in the face. Find ease within the effort."*

---

### Exiting the Pose

1. Bend extended leg, draw knee back in
2. **Maintain the twist** as you lower
3. With control, lower foot to floor
4. Return to Revolved Side Angle, then release bind
5. Unwind slowly — *Tadasana* (Mountain Pose)
6. Pause. Notice sensations. Notice the difference between sides.
7. **Repeat entire sequence on left side**

---

### Teaching Notes for Peak Pose

**Pause Points:**
- After demonstrating each progression, pause for questions
- Walk the room and offer individual guidance
- Remind students: *"The twist is the teacher. The leg extension is extra credit."*

**Common Mistakes Summary:**
| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| Losing twist when standing | Re-establish rotation before lifting higher |
| Rounding spine | Lengthen first, then twist |
| Locking standing knee | Micro-bend for stability |
| Forcing leg straight | Bend knee, prioritize twist and balance |
| Unwinding as leg extends | Reduce extension, deepen rotation |
| Holding breath | Breathe into the compressed side |

**Adjustment Opportunities Summary:**
- *Hands-on:* Support thigh, guide rotation, stabilize standing hip (always with consent)
- *Verbal:* Twist cues, Drishti reminders, permission to use wall/strap

---

## 5. Cool Down (8-10 min)

**Goal:** Counter-pose the twist, release hips and spine, down-regulate

### Gentle Counter-Twist

**Supine Twist — *Supta Matsyendrasana***
- Knees to chest, drop both knees to right
- Arms wide, gaze left (or neutral)
- **Prop Setup:** Bolster or block between/under knees
- Hold 8-10 breaths each side
- Cue: *"After deep twisting, we counter-twist gently. Let the spine neutralize."*

---

### Hip Release

**Reclined Pigeon — *Supta Kapotasana* (Figure Four)**
- On back, right ankle crosses over left thigh
- **Prop Setup:** Strap around left thigh if hands don't reach
- Hold 8-10 breaths each side
- Cue: *"Let the hips release. No more effort needed."*

---

### Hamstring Release

**Reclined Hand-to-Big-Toe — *Supta Padangusthasana***
- Strap around right foot, extend leg toward ceiling
- Keep leg centered (no twist) to neutralize
- Hold 6-8 breaths each side
- Cue: *"Simple and symmetrical. Let the body recalibrate."*

---

### Spinal Neutralization

**Knees to Chest — *Apanasana***
- Hug both knees into chest
- Gentle rock side to side
- Hold 5-6 breaths
- Cue: *"Massage the low back. Return to center."*

---

### Happy Baby — *Ananda Balasana*
- Grab outer edges of feet or use strap around feet
- Gentle rock side to side
- Hold 6-8 breaths
- Cue: *"Playfulness. Softness. Release."*

---

## 6. Savasana + Closing (5-7 min)

### Setup
- *Savasana* (Corpse Pose)
- **Props:** Bolster under knees, blanket for warmth, eye covering offered
- Cue: *"Let the body be completely symmetrical. No more twisting, no more effort."*

### Guided Rest (3-4 min)
- Draw attention to the **spine** — from tailbone to crown
- *"Notice how the spine feels after wringing and releasing."*
- Draw attention to the **sacral area** — lower belly, hips
- *"Notice any sense of spaciousness, of having created room."*
- *"Allow the breath to be completely natural."*
- Silence for 1-2 minutes

### Closing (2 min)
- Invite small movements — fingers, toes
- Knees to chest, roll to right side in fetal position
- *"The fetal position — a gentle twist, a return to origin."*
- Rise to seated

### Seal the Practice
- Hands to heart in *Anjali Mudra*
- Return to the guiding principle:
  > *"To twist is to wring out the old and make space for the new. We revolve to evolve."*
- *"Carry this sense of transformation with you. Remember that growth often comes through spiraling, not just moving forward."*
- Close with *"Namaste"* or moment of gratitude

## Key Differences: Standard vs. Revolved Bird of Paradise

| Aspect | Standard (*Svarga Dvijasana*) | Revolved (*Parivrtta Svarga Dvijasana*) |
|--------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Bind** | Same-side (right arm under right thigh) | Opposite-side (left arm under right thigh) |
| **Entry Pose** | Bound Extended Side Angle | **Revolved** Extended Side Angle |
| **Chest Direction** | Opens toward bound leg side | Rotates **away** from bound leg |
| **Primary Challenge** | Hip opening + balance | Hip opening + balance + **spinal rotation** |
| **Philosophy** | Unfolding, blooming | Transformation, evolution through spiraling |
