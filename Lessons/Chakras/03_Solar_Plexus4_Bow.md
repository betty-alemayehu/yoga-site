# Solar Plexus Chakra — Manipura
**60-Minute Practice**

**Theme:** Inner fire, heart-opening strength, courageous expansion  
**Mantra:** *I ignite my power. I open to possibility.*  
**Element:** Fire  
**Quality:** Radiant, expansive, courageous  
**Peak Pose:** *Dhanurasana* (Bow Pose) with variations  
**Style:** All-levels Hot Yoga | Hatha → Vinyasa progression  
**Music:** Building, uplifting, heart-opening

**Opening Quote (choose one):**  
- "The wound is the place where the Light enters you." — Rumi  
- "You gain strength, courage, and confidence by every experience in which you really stop to look fear in the face." — Eleanor Roosevelt  
- "A strong back and a soft front — that is the warrior's way." — Brené Brown

**Chakra Overview (brief):**  
*Manipura* lives at the solar plexus — the center of willpower, transformation, and personal power. Bow Pose compresses the belly (stoking digestive fire) while opening the chest and shoulders — a balance of strength and surrender. The pose requires courage to lift and expand, embodying the solar plexus invitation to step into your power.

## 1. Opening (4-5 min)

- Welcome, class format (Hatha → Flow)
- Consent cards, props, heat awareness
- **Intention:**  
  *"Today we stoke the fire at the center and let it open the heart. Strength and softness together."*
- Breath awareness at the solar plexus (upper belly)
- Invite personal intention around **courage, expansion, or transformation**

## 2. Warm-Up (8-10 min)

**Goal:** Awaken spine, warm shoulders, introduce backbends

### Supine
- Knees to chest, gentle rock
- *Setu Bandhasana* (Bridge Pose) — 2-3 rounds, hold 4-5 breaths
  - *Cue:* "Press through the feet. Lift the hips, open the chest."
- *Supta Matsyendrasana* (Supine Twist) — L/R, 4-5 breaths each

### Prone (Belly-Down)
- *Salamba Bhujangasana* (Sphinx Pose) — hold 5-6 breaths
  - *Cue:* "Forearms root down. Lift the chest, lengthen the neck."
- *Bhujangasana* (Low Cobra) — 2-3 rounds, 3-4 breaths each
  - *Cue:* "Hands light. Let the back muscles do the lifting."
- *Salabhasana* (Locust Pose) — arms alongside body, lift chest and legs
  - 2 rounds, 4-5 breaths each
  - *Cue:* "This is the back strength we need for Bow."

### Tabletop — *Bharmanasana*
- *Marjaryasana-Bitilasana* (Cat-Cow) — 5-6 rounds
- *Parsva Balasana* (Thread the Needle) — L/R, 4-5 breaths each
  - *Cue:* "Open the shoulders. We'll need this mobility."

**Transition:** *Adho Mukha Svanasana* (Down Dog) → walk to top of mat

---

## 3. Sequence 1 — Build the Fire (10-12 min)

**Focus:** Core + legs integration, heat building, chest opening

### Round 1 — Hatha (3-4 breaths each)

- *Tadasana* (Mountain Pose)
- *Utkatasana* (Chair Pose) — arms overhead, chest lifted
  - *Cue:* "Sink low, lift the heart."
- *Uttanasana* (Forward Fold)
- *Ardha Uttanasana* (Half Lift)
- High Lunge — right foot forward, arms sweep up, slight backbend
- *Virabhadrasana I* (Warrior I) — lift chest, slight backbend
  - *Cue:* "Back heel grounds. Heart lifts."
- Humble Warrior — interlace hands behind back, fold inside front thigh
  - *Cue:* "Shoulders open. Strength and surrender."
- Vinyasa (Plank → Chaturanga → *Urdhva Mukha Svanasana* → *Adho Mukha Svanasana*)
  - Emphasize chest opening in Up Dog

### Round 2 — Vinyasa
- Flow through same sequence, 1 breath per movement
- End with Vinyasa

**Repeat second side (left foot forward)**

*Cue:* "The fire is building. Stay with it."

---

## 4. Sequence 2 — Open the Front Body (10-12 min)

**Focus:** Shoulders, hip flexors, quadriceps (prep for Bow)

### Round 1 — Hatha (3-4 breaths each)

- *Virabhadrasana II* (Warrior II) — right leg forward
- Reverse Warrior — deep side stretch, chest opens
  - *Cue:* "Reach up and back. Open the entire front line."
- *Anjaneyasana* (Low Lunge) — back knee down, arms overhead, gentle backbend
  - *Cue:* "Sink the hips. Lift the heart."
- Low Lunge with Quad Stretch
  - Reach back with right hand, grab left foot (back leg)
  - **Strap option:** Loop strap around back foot if hand doesn't reach
  - Hold 4-5 breaths
  - *Cue:* "This is direct prep for Bow. Open the quad, open the hip flexor."
- *Ardha Hanumanasana* (Half Splits) — counter-stretch
  - Hold 4-5 breaths
- Vinyasa

### Round 2 — Vinyasa
- Flow through, 1 breath per movement

**Repeat second side (left leg forward)**

*Cue:* "We're opening the entire front line — hip flexors, quads, chest. This is Bow Pose preparation."

---

## 5. Sequence 3 — Peak: *Dhanurasana* (Bow Pose) (12-15 min)

**Focus:** Back strength, front body opening, breath in compression

### Warm-Up for Peak (Prone Backbends)

**Locust Pose — *Salabhasana***
- Arms alongside body or interlaced behind back
- Lift chest and legs simultaneously
- Hold 4-5 breaths, 2 rounds
- *Cue:* "Engage the back body. This is the strength that lifts you in Bow."

**Half Bow — *Ardha Dhanurasana***
- Lie on belly, bend right knee, reach right hand back to grab outer ankle
- Left arm extends forward for balance
- Inhale: kick right foot into hand, lift chest and right thigh
- Hold 4-5 breaths
- Release, switch sides
- *Cue:* "One side at a time. Find the balance of effort and ease."

---

### Bow Pose Progression

#### Level 1 — Classic Bow (*Dhanurasana*)

1. Lie on belly, forehead to mat
2. Bend both knees, bring heels toward glutes
3. Reach back with both hands, grab outer ankles (not tops of feet)
4. **Inhale:** Kick feet into hands, lift chest and thighs simultaneously
5. Keep knees hip-width apart (tendency is to splay wide)
6. Shoulders draw back, chest opens
7. Gaze forward or slightly up (not crunching neck back)
8. Hold 5-6 breaths
9. **Exhale:** Slowly release, turn head to one side, rest

**Cues:**
- *"Kick into the hands. Let the legs do the work of lifting the chest."*
- *"Knees stay hip-width. Imagine holding a block between the thighs."*
- *"Breathe into the side ribs — the belly is compressed, so find space laterally."*

**Rest:** *Balasana* (Child's Pose) — 5-6 breaths

---

#### Level 2 — Rocking Bow

1. From full Bow, establish the lift
2. Begin to rock forward and back with breath:
   - **Inhale:** Rock forward (chest lowers toward mat)
   - **Exhale:** Rock back (thighs lower toward mat)
3. 4-6 rocks, maintaining grip on ankles
4. Release, rest in Child's Pose

**Cues:**
- *"Massage the belly. Stoke the digestive fire."*
- *"Keep the breath steady — let it drive the movement."*
- *"This is *Manipura* activation — the fire at your center."*

**Rest:** *Balasana* (Child's Pose) — 5-6 breaths

---

#### Level 3 — One-Legged Bow Variations

1. From full Bow, release right ankle
2. Extend right leg straight back, hovering off the floor
3. Keep left foot kicking into left hand
4. Hold 3-4 breaths
5. Return to full Bow, then switch sides
6. Release, rest

**Cues:**
- *"Asymmetry challenges balance. Stay steady through the center."*
- *"Keep lifting through the extended leg — it's active, not resting."*

**Rest:** *Balasana* (Child's Pose) — 6-8 breaths

---

### Common Mistakes & Corrections

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| Knees splaying wide | Keep knees hip-width, engage inner thighs |
| Grabbing tops of feet | Grab outer ankles for better leverage and wrist safety |
| Only lifting chest, thighs stay down | Kick feet into hands to lift thighs equally |
| Holding breath | Breathe into the side ribs; exhale to deepen |
| Compressing low back | Lengthen tailbone toward heels, engage low belly |
| Neck crunching back | Keep gaze forward, neck long as extension of spine |
| Elbows bending outward | Keep arms straight, shoulders externally rotated |

### Adjustment Opportunities

- **Hands-on (with consent):**
  - Gentle lift under thighs to encourage leg engagement
  - Support at shoulders to guide chest lift
  - Hands on outer knees to cue hip-width alignment
- **Verbal:**
  - "Kick the feet back, not up"
  - "Breathe into the side ribs"
  - "Lengthen the tailbone toward the heels"
  - "Soften the face — find ease in the effort"
  - "The legs are the engine; let them lift the chest"

## 6. Cool Down (8-10 min)

**Goal:** Release back, hips, and front body; down-regulate

### Spinal Neutralization
- *Balasana* (Child's Pose) — extended arms, hold 6-8 breaths
  - *Cue:* "Let the spine return to neutral. Breathe into the low back."

### Twists & Hip Release
- *Supta Matsyendrasana* (Supine Twist) — L/R, 6-8 breaths each
  - *Cue:* "Wring out the spine. Let the exhale release tension."
- *Supta Kapotasana* (Reclined Pigeon / Figure Four) — L/R, 6-8 breaths each

### Front Body Opening (Gentle)
- *Supta Baddha Konasana* (Reclined Bound Angle)
  - Soles of feet together, knees wide
  - Optional: blocks or bolster under knees for support
  - Hold 8-10 breaths
  - *Cue:* "Open the hips, open the heart. Let gravity do the work."

### Final Release
- *Apanasana* (Knees to Chest) — gentle rock
- *Ananda Balasana* (Happy Baby) — 5-6 breaths
  - *Cue:* "Playfulness. Softness. Release."

*Cue:* "The fire has done its work. Let the body integrate."

---

## 7. Closing (5 min)

### Savasana
- *Savasana* (Corpse Pose)
- Optional: bolster under knees to release low back after backbends
- *Cue:* "Let the body be heavy. Let the floor hold you completely."

### Guided Awareness (2-3 min)
- Draw attention to the solar plexus — the space just above the navel
- *"Notice the warmth here. The expansion. The quiet courage."*
- *"You opened the front of the body — the vulnerable side. That takes strength."*
- *"A strong back supports a soft front. You practiced both today."*
- Silence for 1-2 minutes

### Seal the Practice
- Invite small movements — fingers, toes
- Knees to chest, roll to right side
- *"Honor this shape — the return to center."*
- Rise to seated
- Hands to heart in *Anjali Mudra*
- Return to mantra:  
  > *I ignite my power. I open to possibility.*
- *"Carry this courage with you. Strength and softness together."*
- Close with *"Namaste"* or moment of gratitude
