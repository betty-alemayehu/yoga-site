# The Practice — 60 min
All-Levels Hot Yoga | Hatha → Vinyasa
Chakra: Sacral (Svadhisthana)
Theme: Fluidity, sensation, creative flow
Mantra: “I allow myself to feel and flow.”
Peak Pose: Baddha Konasana (Bound Angle / Cobbler’s Pose)
Essence: Warm, watery, adaptable
Music: Earthy, soulful; steady build → soften (Hozier-inspired)

============================================================
10 COMMANDMENTS — THROUGH LINE
1) Intention: Permission to feel and move with ease
2) Sanskrit: Sanskrit + English for each pose
3) Asana: Progressive hip + inner thigh opening toward Bound Angle
4) Adjustments & Assists: Consent-based, primarily in Round 1
5) Masterful Cueing: Wave imagery; ground → pelvis → spine → breath
6) Pranayama: Gentle Ujjayi; longer exhales later
7) Philosophy: Water element — soften resistance, don’t force
8) Music: Supports energy arc; clearly felt
9) Connection: Names used during holds
10) Community: Warm welcome + post-class check-in

============================================================
CLASS FORMAT
============================================================

## 1) OPENING (4–5 min)
- Welcome, name, brief introduction
- Props + consent cards acknowledged
- Explain class structure: Warm Up → 3 Sequences (Hatha / Vinyasa) → Cool Down → Savasana
- Intention + Mantra:
  “Svadhisthana is our water center — sensation, creativity, emotional honesty.
   Today we practice: ‘I allow myself to feel and flow.’”
- Encourage choice, rest, hydration in the heat

------------------------------------------------------------

## 2) WARM UP (8–10 min)
Goal: Spine in all 6 directions + wrists & ankles + breath established  
Start supine → transition to standing

### SUPINE (arrival + sacral awareness)
1) Savasana breath  
   Hands to lower belly / side ribs, introduce gentle Ujjayi  
   Cue: “Feel the breath ripple through the pelvis.”

2) Apanasana (Knees-to-Chest)  
   Rock side-to-side; small ankle circles here  
   (Spinal flexion + ankle warming)

3) Supine Figure-4 Twist (threaded) — L / R  
   Flex/point foot while in the shape  
   (Hip external rotation + spinal rotation + ankle mobility)

### TRANSITION SUPINE → STANDING
4) Roll-through to Forward Fold  
   Unwind → hug knees → roll to side → slow rise  
   Arrive directly in Uttanasana

### STANDING (spinal waves + heat)
5) Standing Half Lift Waves  
   (Ardha Uttanasana ↔ Uttanasana)  
   Inhale lengthen / Exhale soften  
   (Flexion + extension)

6) Supported Standing Backbend  
   Hands to low back or cactus arms; gentle wrist mobility as arms lift  
   (Extension + wrist prep)

7) Standing Side Bends — L / R  
   Option to clasp hands or interlace fingers  
   (Lateral flexion)

Arrive in a bent-knee Forward Fold; shake head, soften jaw  
Re-anchor mantra silently: “I allow myself to feel and flow.”

------------------------------------------------------------

## 3) ENERGETIC BODY (30–35 min)
3 mini-sequences
- Round 1: Hatha (3–4 breaths per pose; props + assists)
- Round 2: Vinyasa (1 breath / 1 movement)
- Each Round 2 ends with a vinyasa (Down Dog lives there)

---

### SEQUENCE 1 — “Stability to Flow”
Purpose: build leg strength and pelvic stability to support deeper hip work later

Round 1 — Hatha
1) Utkatasana (Chair)  
   Block between thighs option
2) Anjaneyasana (Runner’s Lunge, high or low)  
   Heel lifted; grounding through the ball of the foot
3) Virabhadrasana II (Warrior II)  
4) Utthita Parsvakonasana (Extended Side Angle) — block/forearm support  
5) Trikonasana (Triangle) — block under hand

Round 2 — Vinyasa
- Flow the same 5 shapes breath-to-movement  
- End with Vinyasa → Down Dog

---

### SEQUENCE 2 — “Make Space in the Bowl of the Pelvis”
Goal: directly prep adductors + external rotation for Bound Angle

Round 1 — Hatha
1) Anjaneyasana (Low Lunge) — knee down, bolster optional  
2) Ardha Hanumanasana (Half Splits) — blocks  
3) Prasarita Padottanasana (Wide-Leg Forward Fold) — hands to blocks  
4) Skandasana (Side Lunge) — supported, stay higher  
5) Malasana (Yogic Squat) — heels on blanket/block, hands at heart

Round 2 — Vinyasa
- Smooth, side-to-side transitions  
- Emphasis on breath rhythm and fluidity  
- End with Vinyasa → Down Dog

---

### SEQUENCE 3 — “Peak: Bound Angle”
Purpose: seated external rotation + intentional softening

Round 1 — Hatha
1) Dandasana (Staff) — sit on blanket if hips tight  
2) Janu Sirsasana (Head-to-Knee) — Left (strap optional)  
3) Janu Sirsasana — Right  
4) Baddha Konasana (Bound Angle) — upright, hands to ankles/feet  
5) Baddha Konasana Fold (variation)  
   Long spine first; blocks/bolster for support

Peak options
- Beginner: blanket under hips + blocks under knees, stay upright
- Intermediate: gentle hinge forward, 5–8 breaths
- Advanced: deeper hinge, steady breath, optional bind (no forcing)

Round 2 — Seated Vinyasa
- Dandasana → Janu L → center → Janu R → Baddha Konasana
- Breath-led, simple, rhythmic
- Optional Vinyasa to honor template, or direct transition to cool down

------------------------------------------------------------

## 4) COOL DOWN (8–10 min)
Goal: integrate, downshift, prepare for rest
Music softens significantly

1) Supta Baddha Konasana  
   Bolster along spine, blocks under knees  
   Cue: “Let the belly soften; let the breath move freely.”
2) Supta Matsyendrasana (Supine Twist) — L / R
3) Ananda Balasana (Happy Baby) — feet to wall option
4) Natural breath; lengthen exhales

Return to mantra gently:
“I allow myself to feel and flow.”

------------------------------------------------------------

## 5) CLOSING (5 min)
- Savasana with eye masks and prop support
- Quiet body scan: hips → belly → ribs → jaw
- Seal class:
  “May you move through life with soft strength — feeling fully, flowing freely.”
- Close on time; invite post-class connection
