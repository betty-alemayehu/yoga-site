# Solar Plexus Chakra — Manipura
**60‑Minute Morning Practice (6:30am Friendly)**

**Theme:** Waking the inner fire with steadiness  
**Mantra:** *I trust myself.*  
**Element:** Fire  
**Quality:** Gradual heat, contained strength, clear focus

**Opening Quote (choose one):**  
- “Trust yourself. You know more than you think you do.” — Benjamin Spock  
- “Confidence is quiet. Insecurities are loud.” — Unknown  

**Chakra Overview (brief):**  
Manipura is the center of will and self‑trust. In the morning, we don’t force the fire — we **ignite it slowly**, building clarity and confidence layer by layer.

---

## Warm Up (10–12 min)
*Goal: gently wake the core, lubricate the spine, build warmth without shock*

- Savasana → hands on upper belly, slow nasal breath  
- Knees‑to‑Chest → gentle rock; option to keep feet down one at a time  
- Supine Twist (L / R) — longer holds, slow return to center  
- Marching Core (supine, one knee lifts at a time)  
  *Option:* Dead Bug for students already awake  
- Roll to Seated → Seated Side Bend (L / R)  
- Seated Twist (L / R)  
- Tabletop → Cat/Cow (slow, controlled)  
- Bird Dog (option to keep toes down)  
- Down Dog → slow pedal → walk to top of mat

*Cue:* “We’re waking the fire — not rushing it.”

---

## Sequence 1 — Establish Strength
*Focus: legs + core support, accessible entry*

- Chair (Utkatasana)  
  *Option:* hands to thighs, higher stance  
- Chair Twist (Parivrtta Utkatasana)  
  *Option:* keep chest lifted, shallow twist  
- High Lunge (Anjaneyasana)  
  *Option:* shorten stance or lower back knee  
- High Lunge Twist  
  *Option:* hands at heart, elbow to thigh  
- Warrior I (Virabhadrasana I)  
→ **Gentle Vinyasa**  
  (Knees down → Chaturanga optional → Cobra)

Repeat second side with slightly more heat.

---

## Sequence 2 — Contain & Focus
*Focus: confidence through balance and clarity*

- Warrior II (Virabhadrasana II)  
- Reverse Warrior  
- Extended Side Angle  
  *Option:* forearm to thigh or block  
- Half Moon (Ardha Chandrasana)  
  *Option:* block + bent standing leg  
- Warrior III  
  *Option:* fingertips down or stay in High Lunge  
→ **Vinyasa or step‑back transition**

*Cue:* “Power doesn’t wobble — it adapts.”

---

## Sequence 3 — Peak: Core & Will (Morning‑Appropriate)
*Focus: commitment without burnout*

- Forearm Plank  
  *Option:* knees down  
- Side Plank  
  *Option:* bottom knee down or stay in forearm plank  
- Boat (Navasana)  
  *Option:* hands behind thighs  
- Low Boat → High Boat (1–2 slow rounds only)  
  *Option:* stay in static Boat  
- Seated Twist (simple, grounding)

*Peak cue:*  
“Notice what happens when you stay — calmly, deliberately.”

---

## Cool Down (8–10 min)
*Goal: stabilize energy so students leave clear, not depleted*

- Seated Forward Fold  
- Supine Twist (L / R)  
- Reclined Figure‑4  
- Gentle belly breathing, longer exhales

*Cue:* “The fire is lit. It doesn’t need more fuel.”

---

## Closing
- Savasana  
- Brief awareness at solar plexus  
- Seal with mantra: *I trust myself.*
