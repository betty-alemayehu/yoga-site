# Solar Plexus Chakra — Manipura
**60-Minute Practice**

**Theme:** Inner fire, core stability, unwavering focus  
**Mantra:** *I am strong. I am steady. I am enough.*  
**Element:** Fire  
**Quality:** Radiant, stable, empowered  
**Peak Pose:** *Vasisthasana* (Side Plank) with variations  
**Style:** All-levels Hot Yoga | Hatha → Vinyasa progression  
**Music:** Building, empowering, rhythmic

**Opening Quote (choose one):**  
- "You have power over your mind — not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength." — Marcus Aurelius  
- "Strength does not come from physical capacity. It comes from an indomitable will." — Gandhi  
- "The core of your being is the fire that never dies." — Unknown

**Chakra Overview (brief):**  
*Manipura* lives at the solar plexus — the center of willpower, self-discipline, and personal identity. Side Plank embodies this energy: requiring core engagement, mental focus, and the confidence to balance on one hand. When we strengthen this center, we cultivate the inner fire that fuels decisive action and self-trust.

---

## 1. Opening (4-5 min)

- Welcome, class format (Hatha → Flow)
- Consent cards, props, heat awareness
- **Intention:**  
  *"Today we build from the center. The fire at your core is your anchor."*
- Breath awareness at the solar plexus (upper belly)
- Invite personal intention around **strength, stability, or self-trust**

---

## 2. Warm-Up (8-10 min)

**Goal:** Awaken core, warm wrists, introduce lateral body

### Supine
- Knees to chest, gentle rock
- Dead Bug (slow, controlled) — 6-8 reps
  - *Cue:* "Press low back into the mat. Move with control."
- *Supta Matsyendrasana* (Supine Twist) — L/R, 4-5 breaths each

### Tabletop — *Bharmanasana*
- **Wrist Prep (essential for Side Plank):**
  - Wrist circles — 5 each direction
  - Fingers forward stretch — lean gently forward
  - Fingers toward knees stretch — lean gently back
- *Marjaryasana-Bitilasana* (Cat-Cow) — 5-6 rounds
- Bird Dog — hold 3-4 breaths each side
  - *Cue:* "Reach long through fingertips and heel. Fire up the center."

### Plank Prep
- Forearm Plank — 30 sec hold
  - *Cue:* "One long line from crown to heels."
- Forearm Side Plank (knees down) — brief intro, 3-4 breaths each side
  - *Cue:* "This is a preview. We'll build to the full expression."

**Transition:** *Adho Mukha Svanasana* (Down Dog) → walk to top of mat

---

## 3. Sequence 1 — Build the Fire (10-12 min)

**Focus:** Core + legs integration, heat building

### Round 1 — Hatha (3-4 breaths each)

- *Tadasana* (Mountain Pose)
- *Utkatasana* (Chair Pose)
  - *Cue:* "Sink low, lift the chest. Feel the fire in the thighs."
- *Parivrtta Utkatasana* (Revolved Chair) — twist right
- Return to center, twist left
- *Uttanasana* (Forward Fold)
- *Ardha Uttanasana* (Half Lift)
- Step back to High Lunge — right foot forward
- High Lunge Twist — left elbow outside right knee
- *Virabhadrasana I* (Warrior I)
- Vinyasa (Plank → Chaturanga → *Urdhva Mukha Svanasana* → *Adho Mukha Svanasana*)

### Round 2 — Vinyasa
- Flow through same sequence, 1 breath per movement
- End with Vinyasa

**Repeat second side (left foot forward)**

*Cue:* "The heat is building. Stay with it."

---

## 4. Sequence 2 — Lateral Strength (10-12 min)

**Focus:** Side body, obliques, balance prep

### Round 1 — Hatha (3-4 breaths each)

- *Virabhadrasana II* (Warrior II) — right leg forward
- Reverse Warrior — emphasize side stretch
  - *Cue:* "Reach up and back. Feel the entire left side body lengthen."
- *Utthita Parsvakonasana* (Extended Side Angle)
  - Forearm to thigh or hand to block
- *Trikonasana* (Triangle Pose)
  - *Cue:* "This is the lateral line we'll strengthen in Side Plank."
- *Ardha Chandrasana* (Half Moon)
  - Block under bottom hand recommended
  - *Cue:* "Stack the hips. Radiate from the center."
- Vinyasa

### Round 2 — Vinyasa
- Flow through, 1 breath per movement
- End with Vinyasa

**Repeat second side (left leg forward)**

*Cue:* "Feel the side body awakening. This is preparation for our peak."

---

## 5. Sequence 3 — Peak: *Vasisthasana* (Side Plank) (12-15 min)

**Focus:** Core stability, shoulder strength, mental focus

### Warm-Up for Peak

- Plank Hold — 5 breaths
  - *Cue:* "Spread the fingers wide. Press the floor away."
- Plank to Down Dog — 3-4 reps
  - *Cue:* "Warm the wrists. Find the rhythm."

---

### Side Plank Progression

#### Level 1 — Knee Down (Beginners)

1. From Plank, drop right knee to floor (kickstand position)
2. Shift weight into right hand
3. Stack left hip over right
4. Left arm extends to ceiling
5. Bottom knee stays grounded for stability
6. *Drishti* (gaze) to top hand or forward
7. Hold 5-6 breaths
8. Return to Plank, repeat left side

**Cues:**
- *"The knee is your anchor. Build confidence here."*
- *"Lift the hips — don't let them sag."*

---

#### Level 2 — Full Side Plank (*Vasisthasana*)

1. From Plank, shift weight to right hand
2. Roll onto outer edge of right foot
3. Stack feet (or stagger front/back for stability)
4. Left arm extends to ceiling
5. Hips lift, body forms one diagonal line
6. *Drishti* to top hand or forward
7. Hold 5-6 breaths
8. Return to Plank, Vinyasa optional, repeat left side

**Cues:**
- *"Press the floor away. Lift from the bottom oblique."*
- *"Stack the hips like two plates."*
- *"Reach through the crown of the head."*

---

#### Level 3 — Tree Variation (*Vrksasana* in Side Plank)

1. From full Side Plank (right hand down)
2. Bend top (left) knee
3. Place left foot on inner right thigh or calf (not on knee)
4. Top arm can stay extended or come to hip
5. Hold 4-5 breaths
6. Extend leg back to full Side Plank, return to Plank
7. Repeat left side

**Cues:**
- *"Find your Tree Pose balance — now sideways."*
- *"The gaze steadies the mind."*

---

#### Level 4 — Top Leg Lifted (*Vasisthasana* B)

1. From full Side Plank (right hand down)
2. Lift top (left) leg toward ceiling
3. **Option A:** Leg lifts straight up, parallel to floor or higher
4. **Option B:** Bend top knee, grab big toe with top hand (full *Vasisthasana* B)
5. Keep hips lifted, core engaged
6. Hold 4-5 breaths
7. Lower leg, return to Plank
8. Repeat left side

**Cues:**
- *"Only lift as high as you can while keeping hips stacked."*
- *"This is your fire — let it radiate."*

---

#### Level 5 — Wild Thing (*Camatkarasana*) (Advanced)

1. From Side Plank (right hand down)
2. Bend top (left) knee, step left foot behind you
3. Lift hips high, let chest open to ceiling
4. Left arm can reach overhead
5. Hold 3-4 breaths
6. Return through Side Plank to Plank
7. Vinyasa, repeat left side

**Cues:**
- *"Flip your grip, open your heart."*
- *"This is the ecstatic expression — enjoy it."*

---

### Common Mistakes & Corrections

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| Hips sagging toward floor | Engage obliques, lift hips in line with shoulders |
| Bottom shoulder collapsing | Press firmly through hand, externally rotate shoulder |
| Holding breath | Breathe steadily; exhale to engage core deeper |
| Wrist pain | Come to forearm variation or take knee down |
| Neck strain (looking up) | Gaze forward or down if neck is uncomfortable |
| Weight dumping into bottom hand | Distribute weight through entire hand, especially fingertips |

### Adjustment Opportunities

- **Hands-on (with consent):**
  - Support under hips to guide alignment
  - Gentle lift at ribcage to encourage length
  - Stabilize standing shoulder from behind
- **Verbal:**
  - "Fire up the bottom oblique"
  - "Stack the hips like two plates"
  - "Reach through the crown of the head"
  - "Soften the face — strength doesn't require tension"

## 6. Cool Down (8-10 min)

**Goal:** Release wrists, side body, and core; down-regulate

### Wrist Release (Essential)
- Shake out wrists gently
- Wrist stretches — fingers forward, fingers back, prayer stretch
- *Cue:* "Thank your wrists. They held you up."

### Gentle Stretches
- *Balasana* (Child's Pose) — extended arms, rest forehead
  - Hold 6-8 breaths
- Seated Side Stretch — right arm overhead, lean left
  - Hold 5-6 breaths each side
  - *Cue:* "Stretch what we strengthened."
- *Paschimottanasana* (Seated Forward Fold)
  - Hold 6-8 breaths
- *Supta Matsyendrasana* (Supine Twist) — L/R
  - Hold 6-8 breaths each side
- *Ananda Balasana* (Happy Baby)
  - Hold 5-6 breaths
  - Gentle rock side to side

*Cue:* "The fire has done its work. Let the heat settle into the bones."

---

## 7. Closing (5 min)

### Savasana
- *Savasana* (Corpse Pose)
- Hands on upper belly optional — feel the solar plexus
- *Cue:* "Let the body be heavy. Let the floor hold you completely."

### Guided Awareness (2-3 min)
- Draw attention to the solar plexus — the space just above the navel
- *"Notice the warmth here. The steadiness. The quiet power."*
- *"This is your center. It holds you up — on the mat and off."*
- Silence for 1-2 minutes

### Seal the Practice
- Invite small movements — fingers, toes
- Knees to chest, roll to right side
- Rise to seated
- Hands to heart in *Anjali Mudra*
- Return to mantra:  
  > *I am strong. I am steady. I am enough.*
- *"Carry this fire with you. Trust your own strength."*
- Close with *"Namaste"* or moment of gratitude
